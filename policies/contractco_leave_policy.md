# ContractCo Pte Ltd — Leave Policy (Demo)

> Illustrative contractor policy for the hackathon demo.

## Annual Leave
Contractors are entitled to Annual Leave as stated in their individual contract,
typically 14 days per year. Unused leave does not carry over and is not paid out
on contract end unless the contract states otherwise.

## Sick Leave
Contractors receive statutory Sick Leave in line with the Employment Act, with a
valid medical certificate. Hospitalisation Leave is subject to the terms of the
individual contract.

## Not Covered
Contractors are not eligible for Childcare Leave or Off-in-Lieu under this
policy. Questions about entitlements should be directed to the contracting
agency's HR contact.
