## Public holidays

Singapore gazetted public holidays apply. Holidays on a Sunday are observed on the next working day. Holidays on a Saturday earn one day of time off in lieu (see Time off in lieu).

## 1. Annual leave

Entitlement depends on completed years of service and resets on 1 January.

| Completed years of service | Days per year |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 | 14 |
| 2 to 4 | 16 |
| 5 to 9 | 19 |
| 10 to 14 | 22 |
| 15 or more | 24 |

- Leave accrues quarterly: one quarter of your entitlement is credited on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October.
- Up to 5 unused days carry forward to the next year and must be used by 31 March. Anything left after that is forfeited.
- New joiners are pro-rated by full months worked in the first year.
- Leavers are pro-rated to their last day. Days taken beyond the pro-rated amount are deducted from final pay.
- FTH employees follow the same table. Their leave resets on their contract anniversary and does not carry forward.

**Annual leave top-up.** During the open enrolment window each November, you may buy up to 4 extra days for the following year. The cost is your daily rate, deducted from salary over 6 months. The decision cannot be reversed, and top-up days cannot be carried forward. Top-up days are used after regular annual leave.

**Public holiday during leave.** If a holiday falls inside approved leave, the day is credited back automatically. You do not need to cancel.

**Sick during leave.** With an MC, affected days convert to sick leave if you have sick leave remaining.

## 2. Sick and hospitalisation leave

| | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Outpatient sick leave | 16 days per year |
| Hospitalisation leave | 65 days per year, including any sick leave already taken |

- An MC from a registered practitioner is required for every day of sick leave. Give it to your manager within 3 working days of returning.
- If you have used your 16 outpatient days, further outpatient days are unpaid sick leave or annual leave.
- Hospitalisation leave cannot be used for outpatient visits.
- At 55 days of hospitalisation leave, your manager contacts the People Team to discuss support and extended leave.

---

## 3. National Service

Male Singapore citizens and permanent residents called up for reservist training receive paid leave for the call-up period. Share the call-up notice with your manager as soon as you receive it, then apply in Workday.

---

## 4. Time off in lieu

If a public holiday falls on a Saturday, you receive 1 day of time off in lieu. The People Team will announce the date it is credited. Take it within 3 months of the holiday. Unused days lapse.

---

## 5. Birthday and volunteer leave

- **Birthday leave:** 1 day in the month of your birthday, or the month before or after.
- **Volunteer leave:** 2 days per year for volunteering with a registered charity. Add the organisation's name in the request comment.

Neither carries forward.

---

## 6. Marriage and compassionate leave

- **Marriage:** 3 working days for your own legal marriage.
- **Compassionate:** 4 working days for the death of a spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law or sibling.

Both are subject to manager approval and apply to FTH employees.

---

## 7. Family care leave

4 days per year to care for a family member in an emergency or illness. A reason is required. It cannot be used for holidays, to offset a notice period or to carry over to next year.

---

## 8. Childcare leave

| Youngest child | Days per year |
|---|---|
| Under 7 | 6 |
| 7 to 12 | 2 |

- You need at least 3 months of continuous service.
- Age is counted by year of birth.
- Add your child under Personal Information > Dependents in Workday before you apply.
- Days cannot be carried forward or used to offset a notice period.

---

## 9. Parental leave

You need at least 3 months of continuous service for every leave in this section.

| Leave | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Maternity | 16 weeks | Start up to 4 weeks before delivery. First 8 weeks in one block, remaining 8 weeks flexible within 12 months of birth. |
| Adoption | 12 weeks | Applies to adoptive mothers. Use within 12 months of the child's birth. |
| Paternity | 3 weeks | Applies to fathers of newborns and adopted infants. Use within 12 months of the child's birth. |
| Shared parental | 10 weeks | Shared between parents, default 5 weeks each. Agree the split within 4 weeks of birth. |
| Unpaid infant care | 12 days per parent per year | For a child under 2. Agree timing with your manager. |

Government-paid portions require the relevant declaration form. Upload it in Workday under Documents.

---

## 10. Unpaid leave

Up to 20 calendar days, after all annual leave has been used. Needs manager approval and a conversation about business impact. Deducted through payroll.

---

## How to apply in Workday

1. Open **Absence** from the home page.
2. Select **Request Absence** and choose the leave type.
3. Enter the dates, add a comment if the leave type needs one, and attach any documents.
4. Select **Submit**. Your manager receives the request.
5. To cancel an approved request, open it under **My Absence** and select **Correct** or **Cancel**.

## Common questions

**Can I take leave without enough balance?**
Annual leave can go up to 3 days into the next year's entitlement, with manager approval. Anything beyond that is unpaid.

**What happens to my carried-forward days if I resign?**
They are included in your pro-rated balance. Any excess taken is deducted from final pay.

**Who sees my MC?**
Your manager and the People Team. Keep the original for 6 months.

**Can I combine leave types?**
Yes. Annual leave can follow sick leave or parental leave if you have balance remaining.
