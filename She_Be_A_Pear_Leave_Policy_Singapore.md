# She Be A Pear Leave Handbook (Singapore)

*Mock policy for demonstration. She Be A Pear is a fictional company.*

Applies to all She Be A Pear employees based in Singapore. Some employees have a different leave entitlement based on their client assignment. Where this applies, the details are in your offer letter and override this handbook.

---

## Quick reference

| Leave type | Entitlement | Paid | Carry-forward | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Annual leave | Accrues monthly, amount per offer letter | Yes, up to earned balance | No (client rules may differ) | Designated manager |
| Sick leave | 14 days per year | Yes, with valid MC | No | Notify manager |
| Furlough leave | As set by the client | Paid or unpaid | n/a | Designated manager |
| Maternity | Per prevailing Singapore legislation | Yes | n/a | She Be A Pear HR |
| Childcare | 6 days (citizen child under 7), 2 days (non-citizen) | Yes | No | Designated manager |
| Paternity | 2 weeks, government-funded | Yes, capped at $2,500 per week | n/a | Designated manager |
| Adoption | Per prevailing Singapore legislation | Yes | n/a | She Be A Pear HR |

---

## 1. Annual leave

Employees are entitled to paid annual leave during their employment. Leave accrues on completion of each month, on a pro-rata basis.

### Joining mid-month

| Joining date | Leave for that month |
|---|---|
| 1st to 5th | Full month's entitlement |
| 6th to 20th | Half a month's entitlement |
| After the 20th | None |

### Leaving mid-month

| Leaving date | Leave for that month |
|---|---|
| 1st to 5th | None |
| 6th to 20th | Half a month's entitlement |
| After the 20th | Full month's entitlement |

### Rules

- Leave used beyond your earned balance is treated as unpaid leave, with a matching salary deduction.
- Excess leave lapses and cannot be encashed.
- Some clients restrict leave use to the contract period or limit how much can be carried forward. If this applies to you, She Be A Pear HR will tell you during induction.
- Submit your leave request by email to your designated manager, with a copy to She Be A Pear HR, at least 2 weeks before the leave starts.
- Go on leave only after your designated manager has approved it.
- If you are travelling outside Singapore for a personal emergency, tell She Be A Pear HR and your designated manager, and share your overseas contact details.

---

## 2. Sick leave

- 14 days of paid sick leave per year.
- Applies only to illness suffered in Singapore, with a medical certificate (MC) from a Singapore Government-licensed doctor.
- Tell your designated manager by call, SMS or email when you apply.
- Sick leave over your available balance is unpaid. Excess sick leave lapses and cannot be encashed.

**Treated as annual leave instead:**

- Sick leave for medical treatment outside Singapore.
- Sick leave from a traditional Chinese medicine doctor.
- Sick leave without a valid MC.

---

## 3. Furlough leave

Applies only if stated in your offer of employment.

Given market conditions, a client may introduce cost-saving measures that require employees to go on mandatory furlough leave, paid or unpaid.

- **Unpaid furlough:** follow the process your designated manager gives you.
- **Paid furlough:** the days are deducted from your available annual leave balance. If the balance is not enough, the remaining days are unpaid.

---

## 4. Maternity leave

- Eligible female employees with at least 3 months of service receive paid maternity leave under prevailing Singapore legislation.
- Unused leave lapses and cannot be encashed.
- Maternity leave cannot be used to offset your notice period when you leave.

**How to apply:** email She Be A Pear HR with a doctor's certificate showing the expected delivery date, at least 3 months before your leave starts.

---

## 5. Childcare leave and government-paid childcare leave

You need at least 3 months of service.

| Situation | Days per year |
|---|---|
| Singapore citizen child under 7 | 6 days statutory paid childcare leave (Child Development Co-Savings Act) |
| Parent of a non-citizen child covered under the Employment Act | 2 days statutory childcare leave |

- Unused leave lapses and cannot be encashed.
- Childcare leave cannot be used to offset your notice period when you leave.

---

## 6. Paternity leave

- Eligible male employees with at least 3 months of service receive 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, funded by the Singapore Government under prevailing legislation. The exact number of days depends on your working days per week.
- Pay is capped at $2,500 per week, including CPF contribution.
- Paternity leave cannot be used to offset your notice period when you leave.

---

## 7. Adoption leave

- Eligible female employees with at least 3 months of service receive paid adoption leave under prevailing Singapore legislation.
- Unused leave lapses and cannot be encashed.
- Adoption leave cannot be used to offset your notice period when you leave.

**How to apply:** email She Be A Pear HR with your Formal Intent to Adopt and the declaration form (AL1). Do this as soon as you start the adoption process.
