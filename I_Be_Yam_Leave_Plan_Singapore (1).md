## Gazetted Public Holidays - Singapore

Employees covered by the Employment Act is entitled to 11 gazetted public holidays in a year - New Year's Day, Chinese New Year (two days), Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Haji, Good Friday, Labour Day, Vesak Day, National Day, Deepavali and Christmas Day.

Ministry of Manpower (MOM) publishes dates for public holidays one year in advance, usually in the 2nd quarter of the previous year. For example, public holiday dates in 2018 is published in April 2017.

MOM will need to consult the relevant religious boards on the dates for religious public holidays (Good Friday, Vesak Day, Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Haji and Deepavali), in order to arrive at projections of public holiday dates. Some dates are only available by the 2nd quarter of the previous year.

## 1. Vacation - also known as Annual Leave

The I Be Yam Vacation / Annual Leave Plan is based on your years of service in I Be Yam and your entitlement will be reset on every 01 Jan YYYY.

| Years of Service | Days of Vacation |
|---|---|
| 1 – 4 | 15 |
| 5 – 9 | 18 |
| 10 – 19 | 20 |
| 20 – 24 | 23 |
| 25 or more | 25 |

### Entitlement & Accrual

- Your leave entitlement is determined by your total years of service with I Be Yam and is not limited to your employment in I Be Yam Singapore, provided there is no break in service.
- Your annual leave is accrued monthly and is calculated by dividing your total yearly entitlement by 12.
  - Example: If you are entitled to 18 days leave annually, you will accrue 1.5 days per month (18÷12=1.5). This means you gradually build up your leave balance throughout the year.
- There is no carry-forward policy. All unused leave will be forfeited on January 1st of the following year (for regular employee).

### Vacation (Annual Leave) for Fixed-Term Hires (FTH)

- The FTH leave entitlement will also follow the vacation leave entitlement table of regular employees.
- For local-FTH and GI-FTH employees, your leave entitlement will be reset on your contract anniversary date, not on January 1st.
- As there is no carry forward policy, all unused Vacation / Annual Leave will be forfeited on your contract anniversary.
- FTH employees are expected to use their annual leave before the end of their contract. Leave encashment will only apply if there is any remaining balance upon separation.

As an example, if the contract period is 1 Jul 2015 to 30 Jun 2017, your leave period is as follows:

- 1 Jul 2015 to 30 Jun 2016 → all unused Vacation / Annual Leave will be forfeited on 1 Jul 2016.

### FAQ – Vacation (also known as Annual Leave)

**Q1: Can I save my vacation/ annual leave?**

Answer: No, there is no carry-forward policy in I Be Yam. All your vacation days/ annual leave must be cleared by the end of the year for regular employees and at the end of contract anniversary for FTH employees, else they will be forfeited.

**Q2: What if an I Be Yam holiday occurs during my vacation?**

Answer: If an I Be Yam holiday is observed during your vacation period and your leave application has been approved by your manager, you will have to initiate a leave cancellation to reinstate your vacation / annual leave entitlement.

**Q3: What if I fall sick whilst on annual leave? Am I still entitled to paid sick leave?**

Answer: Please initiate a discussion with your manager. If you fall sick during your approved annual leave and issued with medical certificate from certified practitioner, the affected annual leave may be converted to medical leave—provided you have not yet exhausted your 14-day medical leave entitlement. If you have already used up your medical leave entitlement, please discuss alternative arrangements with your manager.

**Q4: If I am resigning from I Be Yam, what is my vacation/ annual leave entitlement?**

Answer: Your vacation/ annual leave entitlement will be pro-rated based on your active period in I Be Yam for the year.

**Q5: I'm an FTH employee. Am I entitled to leave encashment, and how much will I receive?**

Answer: Fixed-Term Hire (FTH) employees are expected to utilize or exhaust their accrued annual/vacation leave before the end of their contract.

- Any unused leave will not be carried over.
- Any remaining annual leave balance at the time of separation will either be paid out or deducted from your final pay, depending on whether it results in a positive or negative balance.

**Q6 (for FTH): In the event that my contract has extended, will my remaining annual leave be carried over or paid out to me?**

Answer: No. If your contract has been extended, any annual leave balance from your current contract will be forfeited. Your new AL entitlement will refresh in reference to your new contract.

- If your 6-month contract is extended to additional of 3 to 12 months), your leave will continue to accrue until the new contract end date, with a maximum accrual cap aligned to a 12-month period.
- Any further extension beyond 12 months will be considered a new contract, and a new leave entitlement will begin from that point.

## 2. Vacation Purchased - during annual enrolment only

The additional Vacation Purchase is offered to our employees during the annual enrollment cycle and is available to those covered under the Flex Benefit Program.

**IMPT:** Must utilize availed "Vacation Purchased" prior to the utilization of accrued statutory "Vacation" leave.

### FAQ – Vacation Purchased

**Q1: What if I'm planning an extended vacation in the year and need more vacation days?**

Answer: Under I Be Yam Flex Benefits Plan, you will be able to opt to purchase more Additional Vacation Days Purchase during annual enrolment which occurs annually in 3rd/4th quarter of the calendar year.

**Q2: What if I do not opt to purchase Additional Vacation Days Purchase at enrollment, but need additional days during the year?**

Answer: If you need more vacation days after Flex enrollment is over, you can still take advance leave of up to five days from next year's entitlement or apply for an unpaid leave.

**Q3: If I opt to buy Additional Vacation Days Purchase, but change my mind after the Flex enrollment period has closed, can I reverse the option?**

Answer: The decision to buy vacation is irreversible.

**Q4: If I cannot use up the Additional Vacation Days Purchase I bought, can I defer them to the following year?**

Answer: All Additional Vacation Days Purchase must be cleared by the end of the year or prior to separation from I Be Yam. If you have any unused Additional Vacation Days Purchase days at the end of the year or prior to separation from I Be Yam, they will be forfeited.

**Q5: How much does it cost to buy Additional Vacation Days Purchase?**

Answer: The price tag associated with buying Additional Vacation Days Purchase is equivalent to you daily rate based on your gross salary as of the month prior to the Flex enrollment period. Deduction will be amortized within 12 months and will be fully recovered from any mid-year separation.

**Q6: If I use my flex credit to purchase leave, what is the tax treatment?**

Answer: Vacation purchased will be deducted from your salary via payroll and it is subjected to tax.

## 3. Medical/Hospitalization Leave

Employees who fall sick during employment are eligible for medical and must inform his/her manager/supervisor on a timely basis.

You are entitled to 14 working days of medical leave (outpatient sick leave) when you are assessed by a registered medical practitioner to be unfit for work due to illness or injuries.

If hospitalization leave is deemed necessary by the doctor, you are entitled to a total of 60 working days of hospitalization leave (inclusive of any medical leaves taken during the year). Should you require extended leave, reach out, or request your manager to contact the Corporate Health & Safety/ Medical Case Management Team on your behalf, for recommendation once sick leave reaches 50 days.

Medical Certificate (MC) is required to support your outpatient/hospitalization sick leave.

### Sequential Leave Utilization

| Leave Type | When | Remarks |
|---|---|---|
| Medical/Hospitalization | The employee is certified to be unfit for work by a medical practitioner registered under the Medical Registration Act or Dental Registration Act. | Employees on hospitalization leave approaching 50 days should seek consultation from Corporate Health & Safety/Medical Case Management should extended leave is needed. |
| Sickness & Accident Income Plan (SAIP) | The employee requires extended leave and has fully exhausted their statutory medical or hospitalization leave. | The employee must secure recommendation from MCM via email for the extended leave. |
| Annual Leave | After the employee has fully availed the leave programs in 1 & 2. | Subject to manager's approval. |
| Additional Time off – Unpaid | After the employee has fully availed the leave programs from 1 to 3. | Maximum of 30 days and is subject to manager's approval. |
| Personal Leave of Absence – Unpaid | The employee has exhausted their accrued annual leave entitlement and requires additional leave for medical reasons. | |
| Medical Disability Plan | The employee is Totally/Permanently Disabled after he/she has been with I Be Yam continuously for 10 years. | The employee must secure recommendation from MCM via email for the extended leave. |

### FAQ – Medical / Hospitalization Leave

**Q1: What is the total number of outpatient and hospitalization sick leave that an employee can take altogether?**

Answer: The total number of entitlements for medical (outpatient) and hospitalization sick leave that an employee can take is capped at 60 days.

Employees who have initially taken the 14 days of medical leave and was deemed necessary to take hospitalization leave would only have remaining 46 days of entitlement to utilize.

**Q2: Are paid medical (outpatient) leave and paid hospitalization leave entitlements interchangeable?**

Answer: No, employees who have already taken all their paid medical leave cannot use their balance paid hospitalization leave entitlements for their outpatient sick leave.

**Q3: Who do I hand my Medical Certificate (MC) to?**

Answer: You are required to submit your MC to your manager to sighting and endorsement. Please keep a copy of the endorsed MC for a period of 6 months. For audit purposes, you may be required to submit the endorsed MC when requested by HR.

**Q4: Am I allowed to take more than 60 days of paid Medica/Hospitalization leave?**

Answer: You or Your manager will need to consult Corporate Health & Safety team or Medical Case Management for review and recommendation.

## 4. In-Camp/Reservist Training - also known as National Service Leave

National Service leave is a compulsory leave taken by every Singapore citizen (male) upon the NS call-ups through the issue of an official call-up notice known as the SAF 100 for SAF NSmen, NP76 for Police NSmen and SCDF 100 for SCDF NSmen. The SAF, SPF and SCDF give about 2 to 6 months of advance notice for NS call-ups. On receipt of the call-up notice, employees are required to inform their manager and apply for leave via SuccessFactors. Employees will be granted leave in accordance with the Enlistment Act and they will be fully compensated accordingly while on leave per the Act.

## 5. Replacement Leave for Public Holidays that Fall on Saturday

Employees are entitled for a replacement leave in-lieu of a public holiday that falls on a Saturday. HR announcement will be sent prior to the Public Holidays that fall on a Saturday to inform employees on how the replacement leave should be managed.

Employees are not required to apply for leave in SuccessFactors (SF) when a Public Holiday falls on a Sunday. In accordance with the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) guidelines under the Employment Act, if a Public Holiday falls on an employee's rest day (Sunday), the next working day will be observed as a paid Public Holiday. Accordingly, the holiday will be reflected in SF, and no leave application is required. Please refer to the Singapore Government Gazette notification for the official Public Holiday calendar.

This leave must be applied in SF under Time Management > Administer Time > Create Absence > Additional Time Off Paid and the comment field must be entered.

**COMMENTS field must be entered**

**Note:** If HR announcement indicates that the Replacement Leave must be taken within the next working day and if employee is required to work due to business need on the next working day; then this is subject to employee manager's approval, alternative arrangements for another date for the replacement leave can be made.

## 8. I Be Yam Family Care Leave

I Be Yam Family Care Leave is also extended to FTH employees, and any unconsumed Family Care leave will lapse at the end of each calendar year. I Be Yam is committed to work/life integration programs that support a flexible work environment, help employees manage work and personal priorities, and enhance employee productivity. The purpose of Family Care Leave is to enable employees to take additional paid time off from work to take care of their family members / family matter. To support the flexible work environment, per the guidelines provided in this document, managers have the discretion to approve additional paid time off for Family Care reasons for an employee.

**Note:**

- Family Care Leave entitlement will be coordinated with employee's Childcare Leave eligibility.
- If an employee falls sick on his family care leave, his absence from work would still be treated as family care leave and not sick leave.
- Family Care Leave is not an automatic entitlement and may be granted at the managers' discretion, a reason for absence is required for all Family Care Leave applications.

### How to apply for Family Care Leave

1. Apply in SF under Time Management > Administer Time > Create Absence > Family Care Paid AND the COMMENTS field must be entered.
2. There is no need to upload document for Family Care Leave, simply click "Submit" at bottom of message.
3. Manager's Approval or Rejection

### FAQ – I Be Yam Family Care Leave

**Q1: Can I apply for family care leave if I would like to bring my family for vacation?**

Answer: Family care leave cannot be used for family vacation related purposes. This leave is meant for taking care of family members in event of emergency or sickness.

**Q2: Can I apply for family care leave to accompany my dependents to the doctor?**

Answer: Yes, you can apply family care leave to accompany your dependents to the doctor.

**Q3: My family is overseas (hometown) and my dependent is not feeling well. Can I use my family care to visit them?**

Answer: Yes, you can apply family care leave to the said purpose.

**Q4: Can my manager reject my family leave application?**

Answer: Family care leave is not an automatic entitlement and as per other leave types, leave application are subject to approval by manager. Therefore, manager can reject the application if they have valid reasons to do so.

**Q5: Does my manager need to know the reason when I apply for Family Care Leave?**

Answer: Yes, your manager has the right to know the reason for applying family care leave.

**Q6: Can I use my family care leave in a block?**

Answer: The duration of family leave will depend on the situation of family matters that you need to attend. Please share with your manager the reason for applying family care leave and he will need to assess the situation on a case-by-case basis.

**Q7: Can I use family care leave to off-set my notice period?**

Answer: You cannot use family care leave to offset the notice period when you leave your job.

**Q8: Can I bring forward the unused family care leave?**

Answer: Unconsumed family care leave for the year cannot be carried over to the next year.

## 9. Childcare Leave (Below 7 years old) OR Extended Childcare Leave (7 to 12 years old)

Childcare Leave and Extended Childcare Leave are also available to FTH employees.

### Eligibility

You must have been with I Be Yam Singapore for a continuous period of at least 3 months.

### Entitlement

**Childcare Leave (below 7 years old)**

- If your youngest child is below 7 years old, you are entitled to 6 days of paid childcare leave.
- You get 6 days per year of paid childcare leave until the year your child turns 7 years old, regardless of the number of children you have. The first 3 days paid by I Be Yam and the remaining 3 days covered by the Government.
- You need to consume your yearly childcare leave entitlement by the end of that leave year.
- You cannot carry forward childcare leave meant for one year to the next.

**Extended Childcare Leave (7 – 12 years old)**

- If your youngest child is between 7 – 12 years old, both inclusive, you are entitled to 2 days of paid childcare leave. The 2 days of childcare leave are paid by the Government.
- You need to consume your yearly childcare leave entitlement by the end of that leave year.
- You cannot carry forward childcare leave meant for one year to the next.

### Important Note

- Your child's age is determined by the year your child was born in. Example, if your child is born in YR2020, your child will be considered 7 years old in YR2027, regardless of the month or day your child was born.
- Your leave benefits for subsequent years are based on when you took your first childcare leave.
- If you have more than one child, the leave benefits you can enjoy is not cumulative but is based on your youngest child's age.
- You cannot use childcare leave to offset the notice period when you leave your job.

## 10. Maternity Leave (GPML)

Maternity Leave is available to all female employees of I Be Yam Singapore (inclusive of FTH), regardless of nationality, are entitled to 16 calendar weeks of maternity leave.

### Eligibility

- You must have been with I Be Yam Singapore for a continuous period of at least 3 months immediately before the birth of your child.
- You are lawfully married* to your child's father. *Not applicable for mothers whose children are born (or with Estimated Delivery Date (EDD)) on or after 1 Jan 2017.

### You can plan your leave based on the following

| Arrangement | 16 weeks (includes non-working days, rest days and public holidays) |
|---|---|
| By Default | Take the 16 weeks continuously, starting 4 weeks before delivery. |
| By Mutual Agreement | Start anytime within 4 weeks before delivery. Example: If the expected date of delivery is 7 July, you can start your leave between 9 June and 7 July.<br>You must take the first 8 weeks in one continuous stretch.<br>You can take the last 8 weeks flexibly over 12 months from your child's birth.<br>Example: If the employee worked 5 days in a week, the number of maternity leave days that you can take flexibly is: 8 weeks x 5 days = 40 working days. If you decided to take 2 days of maternity leave per week, the 40 days ÷ 2 maternity leave days per week = 20 weeks. |

### Important Note

- You can start your maternity leave 4 weeks before the delivery date and no later than the delivery date. All maternity leave must be taken within 12 months from your child's date of birth (inclusive of date of birth)
- Before taking your leave, you must inform your employer as soon as possible, at least 1 week before starting your leave. You should reach an agreement with your employer on how and when you intend to take the leave as this will allow them to better plan for work arrangements.
- You cannot use your maternity leave to offset the notice period when you leave your job.
- Mothers who had stillbirths are also eligible for GPML if the requirements are met.

Detailed regulatory description of this leave type is available here.

**If your newborn child is a non-Singapore Citizen**

1. Apply in SuccessFactors under Time Management > Administer Time > Create Absence → Maternity Leave (First 8 Weeks)

### FAQ – Maternity Leave (GPML)

**Q1: I am expecting twins. Are my maternity leaves doubled?**

Answer: As twins are considered as one birth, no, you will not receive double benefits for the birth of your twins.

**Q2: My baby is expected to be due before I complete 90 days service with I Be Yam, am I entitled to maternity leave benefits?**

Answer: If you have worked less than 3 months at the birth of your child, you are eligible for 12 weeks unpaid maternity leave.

**Q4: Can I choose not to take the full maternity leave?**

Answer: Yes, you may choose not to take the full maternity leave. However, you will not be given additional salary in-lieu of the maternity benefits. Alternatively, you could consider taking the last 8 weeks of your maternity leave flexibly within 12 months from delivery.

You should discuss with your manager or HR to come to an agreement on the arrangement that best meets the needs of both parties before you go on maternity leave.

**Q5: I had a miscarriage. Am I eligible for maternity leave?**

Answer: No, GPML does not apply to miscarriage cases.

**Q6: If my baby was a stillborn or passes away subsequently, will I still qualify for maternity leave?**

Answer: Yes, GPML will be extended to mothers with stillbirths (with a notification of still birth issued by the hospital).

If all the eligibility criteria are met but the infant passes away, mothers can still claim for Maternity Benefit on compassionate grounds. Claims will have to be made within 15 months from the birth of the child.

## 11. Adoption Leave (AL)

### Eligibility

- You must have been with I Be Yam Singapore for a continuous period of at least 3 months immediately before your formal intent to adopt.
- You are the child's adoptive mother.
- The adoption order must be passed within 1 year from the formal intent to adopt.
- You are/have been lawfully married at the point of your formal intent to adopt.
  - Not applicable for adoptive mothers whose formal intent to adopt is on or after 1 January 2017.

### Entitlement

- You can start your adoption leave from your formal intent to adopt.
- You must consume your adoption leave before the child's first birthday.

### Important Note

- All adoption leave must be taken within 12 months from your child's Date of Birth (inclusive of Date of Birth).
- You cannot use your adoption leave to offset the notice period when you leave your jon.

Formal intent to adopt is defined based on the following:

| Child's citizenship at Date of Birth | Definition of "Formal Intent to Adopt" |
|---|---|
| Singapore Citizen | Date when an application to adopt is made to the Court. |
| Non-Singapore Citizen | Date when the Dependant's Pass (DP) is issued in respect of the children document indicating approval. |

- If your adoptive child is not a Singapore Citizen, your adopted child must obtain Singapore citizenship within 6 months after the Adoption Order is passed.
- The Government will recover reimbursements from you if the Adoption Order is not granted within 1 year from your formal intent to adopt (inclusive of formal intent to adopt) or if your adopted child (if not a Singapore Citizen) does not obtain Singapore Citizenship within 6 months after the Adoption Order is passed

## 12. Paternity Leave (GPPL)

I Be Yam recognizes that fathers play an important role in raising children.

To emphasize the importance of shared parenting responsibility, and to support fathers in bonding with and caring for their newborn, with effect from 1 Jan 2016, all male employees of I Be Yam Singapore (inclusive of FTH), regardless of nationality, will receive 4 calendar weeks Paternity Leave instead of the regulatory 2 weeks as prescribed by Government.

This leave is also applicable to working father who are adopting an infant.

### Eligibility

**Newborn**

- You must have been with I Be Yam Singapore for a continuous period of at least 3 months immediately before the birth of your child.
- You are/were lawfully married to the child's mother at some point between conception and birth.

**Adopted Infant**

- You must have been with I Be Yam Singapore for a continuous period of at least 3 months immediately before the date of your formal intent to adopt.
- You are the child's adoptive father.
- Your adoptive child is less than 12 months old when you take your adoption leave.
- You are/have been lawfully married* at the point of your formal intent to adopt.

You can take your leave as follows: https://example.com/paternity-leave

### How to apply Parental Leave – GPPL – in one continuous block

**If your newborn child or adopted infant is a Singapore Citizen**

*Taken In One Continuous Block*

1. Download and complete Paternity Leave here.
2. Apply in SF under Time Management > Administer Time > Create Absence > Parental Time Off. Upload your completed Paternity Leave (for Benefits Team to seek reimbursement from Government).
3. Manager's approval or rejection.

*Taken Flexibly*

1. Download and complete the Parental Leave form here.
2. SF under Time Management > Administer Time > Create Absence > Parental Time Off
3. Go to "Maintain My Worker Documents".
4. Upload your completed Parental Leave form (for Benefits Team to seek reimbursement from Government).
5. Manager's approval or rejection.

**If your newborn child or adopted infant is a Non-Singapore Citizen**

With effect from 1 January 2016, in line with our continued focus on supporting employees in work/life integration, I Be Yam has enhanced our adoption policy to extend both parental and adoption leave to non-Singaporean male employees.

1. Apply in SF under Time Management > Administer Time > Create Absence > Parental Time Off
2. Manager's approval or rejection.

### FAQ – Parental Leave (GPPL)

**Q1: My wife had a stillbirth. Am I eligible for paternity leave?**

Answer: Yes, if you meet the eligibility criteria for paternity leave and include a notification of still birth issued by the hospital.

**Q2: My wife had a miscarriage. Am I eligible for paternity leave?**

Answer: No, this is having the same treatment for GPML which does not apply to miscarriage cases.

## 13. Shared Parental Leave (SPL)

Eligible working parents can enjoy SPL, to be shared between parents and taken either in a continuous block or flexibly within 12 months from child's date of birth inclusive of date of birth.

- If your child's date of birth, estimated delivery date, or date of formal intent to adopt is between 1 July 2017 and 31 March 2025, both dates inclusive, you may be eligible for the 2013 – Shared Parental Leave scheme. Mothers can share up to 4 weeks of Government Paid Maternity Leave with their husbands.
- If your child's date of birth, estimated delivery date or date of formal intent to adopt is between 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026, both dates inclusive, you are entitled to 6 weeks of SPL to be shared between parents. Each parent gets 3 weeks by default.
- Effective 1 April 2026, you are entitled to 10 weeks of shared parental leave to both male and female employees who have newborn. Each parent gets 5 weeks by default.

### Biological parent with live birth

1. You have served I Be Yam Singapore for a continuous period of at least 3 months before the birth of your child.
2. Your child was born, or has an Estimated Delivery Date (EDD), on or after 1 April 2025.
3. All mothers are eligible for SPL. Fathers will be eligible for SPL if they were married to the child's mother when the child was born. If not, the child's father must meet one of the following conditions:
   - They were married to the mother at some point between conception and the child's birth (even if they are no longer married at the time of birth).
   - They marry the mother within 12 months from child's birth, including the date of birth. The father can only start consuming SPL after the date of marriage.
4. For single mothers, all mothers will be eligible for Shared Parental Leave scheme regardless of their marital status. They will be entitled to take the full duration of SPL to support their caregiving needs. Mothers will also be able to share their leave with the child's fathers following a marriage within the first year of the child's birth.
5. For widowed parents, they are entitled to utilise any unconsumed SPL (in blocks of full weeks) that was allocated to their deceased spouse. If a widowed parent wishes to adjust the sharing arrangement after the first 4 weeks of the child's birth will not be required to seek employer's agreement to do so.
6. For divorced parents, married couples who are divorced will remain eligible for SPL if they were lawfully married at some point between conception and before the child's birth, regardless of whether the father is still married to the mother at the time of the child's birth.

### Adoptive parent

1. You have served I Be Yam Singapore for a continuous period of at least 3 months before the birth of your child.
2. Your Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date is on or after 1 April 2025. Please click here to understand the definition of "Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA)".
3. Your child is below 12 months on your FIA date.
4. You are not the biological father or mother of the child.

In the unfortunate event that the child has passed away, the remaining unused SPL will no longer be available for parents to consume. Appeals will be considered on a case-by-case basis. Should parents require additional time away from work, they are encouraged to discuss compassionate leave with their employer or utilize their annual leave entitlements.

### How to apply

Please ensure that your child or adopted child is updated into SuccessFactors and the required supporting documents are uploaded. To do so, please login to SuccessFactors to update the dependent's information. For an adopted child, please select "Child" in the relationship field and use the Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date as the child's Date of Birth. A request will be submitted to create a template for Adopted Child in SuccessFactors.

Once your dependent was successfully added into SuccessFactors, the shared parental leave will appear in the selection of leaves in SuccessFactors.

1. Discuss your sharing arrangement (i.e. number of weeks of SPL allocated to each parent) with your spouse. Notify your manager(s) on your sharing arrangement and leave plans early.
2. Allocate sharing arrangement in LifeSG during birth registration or continue with the default sharing arrangement of 3 weeks for each parent. Parents can change sharing arrangements in LifeSG freely within 4 weeks of child's birth. This step only applies to child who is a Singapore citizen or becomes a Singapore citizen within 12 months from date of birth (inclusive of date of birth).
3. Download declaration form (SPL), complete and submit to AskHR and apply in SuccessFactors.
4. Manager's approval or rejection.

### FAQs

**Q1. If my spouse is a stay-at-home parent or unemployed, will I still be eligible for the full duration of Shared Parental Leave?**

A1: Yes. If one spouse is a stay-at-home parent or unemployed, the working spouse is entitled to the full duration of the SPL, if he or she meets the eligibility criteria. This is to encourage shared parental responsibility, where the working spouse should also have time away from work to care for and bond with the newborn. Employee needs to have early discussions on leave plans and include understanding the employment status of the employee's spouse, so that managers are aware of the employee's entitlement. The working spouse should update his/her sharing arrangement accordingly to reflect his/her own entitlement to take the full 6 weeks of SPL.

**Q2. Will single parents be eligible for Shared Parental Leave (SPL)?**

A2: For single mothers, all mothers will be eligible for Shared Parental Leave scheme regardless of their marital status. They will be entitled to take the full duration of SPL to support their caregiving needs. Mothers will also be able to share their leave with the child's fathers following a marriage within the first year of the child's birth.

For single fathers, SPL will not be automatically extended to single fathers. Appeals will be considered on a case-by-case basis. All single parents remain eligible for Unpaid Infant Care Leave, Childcare Leave & Extended Childcare Leave to support their caregiving responsibilities.

**Q3. Will both parents' agreement be required to change the sharing arrangement?**

A3: Married couples eligible for SPL should discuss and consent to any change to the sharing arrangement. After reaching a mutually agreed, either parent can initiate the change to the sharing arrangement on LifeSG.

**Q4. Will employers' agreement be required to change the sharing arrangement?**

A4: Eligible parents have four weeks from their child's birth or Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date to decide and submit their sharing arrangement. We encourage parents to take this time to carefully consider and discuss their preferred leave arrangements. Parents who have not decided on their sharing arrangement within the first four weeks may consider relying on the default sharing arrangement of sharing the leave equally between parents.

After the first four weeks of their child's birth or FIA date, parents who wish to change their sharing arrangement can only do so if they have obtained their employers' agreement. This is to ensure that both employers are agreeable to the changes and can make the necessary manpower adjustments to manage their employees' absences from work. Parents will need to submit documentation indicating that their employer is agreeable to the sharing arrangement, when making the changes on LifeSG.

**Q5. Can Shared Parental Leave (SPL) be carried over if a parent's employment is terminated and begins working for a new employer?**

A5: Parents whose employment is terminated before fully utilising their Shared Parental Leave (SPL) will not be able to transfer any unutilised leave to their new employer. However, they may reallocate the remaining entitlement, in whole weeks, to their spouse. As this reallocation requires a change in the sharing arrangement, parents must seek their employers' agreement (where applicable) if the change occurs after the first four weeks of the child's birth or Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date.

- **Resignation:** If a parent resigns and their spouse's employer does not agree to the change in sharing arrangement, the remaining SPL will be forfeited.
- **Retrenchment:** Parents who are retrenched may obtain the employer's agreement to reallocate the remaining SPL to their spouse (if applicable) or claim the unutilised entitlement as Shared Parental Leave Benefit (SPB) top-up.
- **Contract expiry:** Parents whose contracts have expired may obtain the employer's agreement to reallocate the remaining SPL to their spouse (if applicable), or claim unutilised entitlement as SPB top-up.
- **Dismissal:** If a parent is dismissed and their spouse's employer does not agree to the change in sharing arrangement, the remaining SPL will be forfeited. Parents who consider themselves to be wrongfully dismissed may seek redress by filing a claim at the Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM).

## 14. Unpaid Infant Care Leave (ICL)

Effective October 31, 2008, Regular and FTH employees were entitled to 6 days of unpaid infant care leave. This was later updated, and starting January 1, 2024, unpaid infant care leave increased from 6 days to 12 days per parent per year for each eligible parent. This means that each parent will have 12 days of unpaid infant care leave per year, in the child's first 2 years, regardless of the number of children.

Detailed regulatory description of this leave type is available at https://example.com/unpaid-infant-care-leave

## 15. Additional Time Off Unpaid

This program is also known as Personal Short Term Unpaid Leave. If after the utilization of all their annual leave entitlement requires additional leave that is equal to or less than 30 calendar days, employees are eligible to apply for Personal Short Term Unpaid Leave, subject to the following:

- All available annual leave has been fully utilized/ exhausted.
- Discussion with your manager.
- Minimal to zero impact to Business

This leave must be applied in SF under Time Management > Administer Time > Create Absence > Additional Time Off Unpaid. Once approved, your unpaid leave will be deducted via Payroll.

Additional Time Off – Unpaid is not an automatic entitlement and may be granted at the managers' discretion.
