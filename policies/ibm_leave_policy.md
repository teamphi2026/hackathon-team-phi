# IBM Singapore — Leave Policy (Demo)

> Illustrative policy for the hackathon demo. Not an official IBM document.

## Annual Leave
Employees accrue Annual Leave (AL) monthly based on years of service: 0–3 years
receive 15 days per year, 4–7 years receive 18 days, and 8 or more years receive
21 days. Unused Annual Leave expires on 31 December with no carry-over into the
next year. Annual Leave requests require manager approval.

## Childcare Leave
Employees with a child aged 7 or under at 1 January are entitled to 6 days of
Childcare Leave per year. Employees whose youngest child is aged between 8 and 12
are entitled to 2 days of Extended Childcare Leave per year. Eligibility is
derived from the dependents on record and resets on 1 January.

## Sick Leave and Hospitalisation Leave
Outpatient Sick Leave (SL) provides 14 days per year and requires a medical
certificate. Hospitalisation Leave (HL) provides 46 days on top of Sick Leave,
for a combined total of 60 days, and requires hospital discharge documentation.
Neither requires prior manager approval.

## Off-in-Lieu
Off-in-Lieu (OIL) is earned for approved work on rest days or public holidays.
Each OIL credit expires three months after it is earned. OIL requests require
manager approval.

## Approval and Coverage
Leave requests route to the employee's direct manager for approval. Managers
consider team coverage: each team has a minimum number of staff who must remain
on duty each working day. Requests that would drop a team below its minimum may
be declined or rescheduled.
